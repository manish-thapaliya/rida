About RIDA

Pioneering Research for Development

RIDA is a Nepal-based international research firm with extensive expertise in research, evaluation, assessment, and advocacy. For over four decades, we have been at the forefront of generating evidence-based insights that shape development policies and programs.

Our mission is to help government and non-state actors design, implement, and evaluate development initiatives that create lasting impact. We combine rigorous methodology with deep contextual understanding to deliver actionable insights.

Areas of Focus

We bring specialized expertise across the full spectrum of development research and analysis.

Development Policy Research & Advocacy

Evidence-based policy analysis and strategic advocacy to influence development agendas at national and regional levels.

Monitoring and Evaluation

Qualitative & Quantitative Research
