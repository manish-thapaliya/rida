Who We Are

Pioneering Development Research Since 2007

Research Inputs and Development Action Pvt. Ltd. (RIDA) is a Nepal based research agency established 2007 A.D. with the key objective to provide research support to development interventions to ensure evidence led development practices.

Our Vision

Promoting evidence-based planning and implementation of development interventions.

Our Mission

Empowering people by improving socio-economic conditions through research in education, health, economy, and IT.

What We Do

Core Service Expertise

Development Policy Research

Evidence-based policy analysis and strategic advocacy to shape development agendas.

Monitoring & Evaluation

Comprehensive M&E frameworks, Baseline, Endline, and Third-Party Monitoring.

Quantitative & Qualitative Research

Behavior Change Communication
