About RIDA

Pioneering Evidence-Based Development Research

Research Inputs and Development Action (RIDA) is a Nepal-based international research firm established in 1980 and registered as a Private Limited company on August 10, 2007. We specialize in research, evaluation, assessment, and advocacy for development initiatives.

Our Vision

To promote evidence-based planning and implementation of development interventions through research inputs at various stages of the project cycle.

Our Mission

Empowering people and communities by improving their socio-economic condition in an equitable way through research and action in education, health, economy, and information technology.

Global Presence

Operating across three major regions with strategic international offices.

Our Expertise

Core Service Offerings

Comprehensive research and consulting services tailored to development challenges.

Development Policy Research & Advocacy

Evidence-based policy analysis and strategic advocacy to shape development agendas at national and regional levels.

Monitoring and Evaluation

Comprehensive M&E services including Baseline, Endline, and Third-Party Monitoring to measure program effectiveness.

Qualitative & Quantitative Research

Rigorous mixed-methods research combining statistical analysis with in-depth qualitative insights for comprehensive understanding.

Behavior Change Communication

Strategic BCC interventions designed to influence behaviors and improve development outcomes in health, education, and livelihoods.

Financial, Economic & Social Analysis

In-depth analysis of economic and social dynamics to inform investment decisions and policy frameworks.

Identification of Poor & Proxy Means Testing

Scientific identification methodologies and targeting mechanisms for social protection programs.
